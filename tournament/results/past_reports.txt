# Past Tournament Reports (Domino Version 2 vs Stockfish 16.1)

## 1. Test Match (10+0.1, Stockfish Skill 3 ~1500)
- Result: Domino swept 2-0.
- Note: Initial test to ensure the engine and cutechess were hooked up correctly.

## 2. Bugged Match (10+0.1, Stockfish Skill 4 ~1700)
- Result: 11 - 9 - 0 (Domino won +34.9 Elo)
- Note: This was run *with* the protected pawn bonus. The massive bonus caused Domino to play very passively (turtle chess), dropping its estimated rating to ~1735.

## 3. Fixed Match (10+0.1, Stockfish Skill 4 ~1700)
- Result: 17 - 3 - 0 (Domino won +301 Elo)
- Note: Ran after removing the protected pawn bonus. The engine played highly aggressively, estimating its rating at ~2000 Elo.

## 4. Full Classical Match (60+0.5, Stockfish Skill 4 ~1700)
- Result: 36 - 3 - 1 (Domino won +407 Elo)
- Note: Ran at a much longer time control (1 minute + 0.5s increment). Domino performed exceptionally well, breaking an estimated ~2100 Elo.

*Note: Since these past games were run before PGN logging was enabled, the move-by-move data for these specific games was not saved. Future games will be saved as PGNs.*
